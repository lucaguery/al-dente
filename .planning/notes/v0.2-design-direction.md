# v0.2 Design Direction

Output of the exploration session that preceded `/gsd-new-milestone v0.2`. These decisions are the locked creative direction for the polish milestone — every UI-SPEC contract and every phase plan should cite this document and resist re-litigating it.

## Principles (user's framing)

The design must satisfy four principles:

- **Design Quality** — coherent whole, not a collection of parts
- **Originality** — deliberate creative choices, no AI-generated patterns (no purple gradients on white cards, no unmodified shadcn defaults)
- **Craft** — typography hierarchy, spacing consistency, color harmony, contrast ratios
- **Functionality** — usability independent of aesthetics

## Decisions

### Mood / identity
**Slow Food artisanal — warm, intimate, but restrained.** Reference points: contemporary Italian craft food publishing (Phaidon-adjacent but earthier), Tuscan cookbook aesthetic, slow-food artisanal brands. Not editorial-cold, not modernist-graphic, not trattoria-themed.

### Italian heritage lean
**Explicit lean.** The name "Al Dente" carries Italian/pasta connotations and we're using them — but the heritage shows up in palette and typography, not in trattoria-themed chrome. No checkered tablecloths, no flag-color combos, no italic-flourish-overload.

### Texture anchor
**Paper-grain on card surfaces.** A single textural move: subtle warm noise/grain on card backgrounds, suggestive of recipe cards on a kitchen counter. Implementation cost: ~30 lines of CSS plus one SVG noise asset. Used consistently across all card surfaces; not used on full-page backgrounds, buttons, or chrome.

### Color anchor
**Terracotta as primary accent**, with cream + ink + warm grays as supporting neutrals.

- Primary actions, active states, emphasis: terracotta (starting point ~`#C8553D`, refine in design-system phase)
- Surfaces: warm cream (NOT shadcn slate/zinc)
- Body text: deep ink, not pure black
- Secondary chrome: warm grays (warm taupe family, NOT cool grays)
- The palette must accommodate food photography sitting next to interface chrome — chrome recedes

### Typography (NOT yet chosen)
Because texture is restrained and color is restrained, **typography becomes the actual signature**. The display serif + body sans pairing is the highest-leverage decision still open. See research question `.planning/research/questions.md` for constraints.

### Motion (NOT yet chosen)
Framer-motion is in the stack and budgeted for the swipe deck and screen transitions. Motion philosophy needs to be picked during the design-system phase: one curve, two durations, sparing decorative use.

## Anti-patterns (committed)

These are explicit "no"s. Future phases that drift into them should be corrected against this document.

- No purple gradients on white cards
- No unmodified shadcn defaults — every component in `components/ui/*` gets re-themed
- No cool grays (slate/zinc family) on surfaces — warm grays only
- No "lean handmade" overload — paper-grain is the *only* textural element; no hand-drawn dividers, no notebook ruled lines, no italic ornamental asides as decoration
- No purely Geist or Geist+Inter pairing — that's the AI-generated default and explicitly fails the Originality principle
- No trattoria theming — checkered patterns, flag colors, ornamental flourishes, etc.
- No clinical / modernist-graphic direction — Vignelli-inspired is wrong for this app
- No themed/restaurant-y chrome that fights food photography content

## Path declined (for the record)

We explicitly considered and declined the **lean handmade** texture path — multiple textural elements, hand-drawn dividers, ornamental italic asides. Reason: effort budget (solo dev, ~1 weekend/week, ~230 hours total). Captured as seed `handdrawn-signature-anchor.md` for revisit after v0.2 ships.

## Implementation hooks

- Tokens live in `frontend/app/globals.css` under Tailwind v4 `@theme` directive
- shadcn components in `frontend/components/ui/*` are modified in place — paste-in becomes paste-then-customize
- Fonts loaded via `next/font/google` with `display: swap`
- French diacritic rendering must be verified at PWA-compressed iOS Safari sizes — this is a real constraint for the type pairing decision
