<objective>
Move the recipe-thread surface from inline-at-bottom-of-structured-view to its own dedicated `/recipes/[id]/thread` route. The structured view (`/recipes/[id]`) loses its inline thread mount (hard deletion, no shim per MVP posture) and gains a tappable `N tours` pin in the `det-top` slot that routes to the new thread page. The thread route shows the existing `<RecipeThread mode="detail" />` under a thin top-bar.

Purpose: The current bottom-of-page thread fights with the structured view for vertical space and breaks the visual hierarchy of the La Grille register. Splitting it into a dedicated route makes both surfaces breathe and gives the `N tours` counter a real navigational role instead of being a passive meta-strip element. Per ADR-0001, raw inputs preserved in `recipe_turns` are the durable store — this plan only changes the surface, not the data contract.

Output: One new route page, one new thin top-bar component, a hard-deleted inline thread render on the structured view, one new `det-top` pin, one new Playwright spec, plus two i18n keys for the back-arrow aria-label and the thread crumb separator.
</objective>
