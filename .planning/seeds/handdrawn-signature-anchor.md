# Hand-drawn signature anchor

## What

The "lean handmade" texture direction we considered and declined for v0.2: hand-drawn dividers between sections (squiggle, brushstroke, ornamental glyph), possibly hand-drawn icons replacing Lucide defaults. This would replace or augment the paper-grain texture chosen for v0.2.

## Why declined now

Effort budget. Requires drawing or commissioning ~6–8 SVG assets, plus ongoing discipline to keep them stylistically consistent. Solo dev with ~1 weekend/week and ~230 hours total can't credibly land this on top of paper-grain + typography + token work without sacrificing functional polish.

## Why revisit later

This was the *highest-ceiling* originality choice we considered. Paper-grain is a vibe additive — a hand-drawn signature is a screenshot-able identity that would make Al Dente immediately recognizable and would defensibly satisfy the Originality principle at the highest level.

## Trigger conditions

Surface this seed when any of these become true:

- v0.2 ships and the design lands at "competent restrained" but the user wants to push to "memorable distinctive"
- Productize-later work begins (broader audience = higher originality bar)
- After 2+ weeks of daily use the app feels too generic or forgettable
- A v0.3+ milestone explicitly tackles "identity / brand"

## What to do when triggered

1. Decide: replace paper-grain or augment it?
2. Source: draw in-house (Procreate / iPad), commission (Fiverr / freelance illustrator), or generate via AI then heavily refine?
3. Scope: dividers only? Add hand-drawn icons? Add ornamental glyphs at section ends?
4. Style guide: thickness, color (likely terracotta or ink), variation (3 squiggles vs 8?), placement rules
